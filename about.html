Who are We

KLG Scientific and Electronic Services was started by professionals with more than 25 years of experience in maintaining different Analytical, Vacuum, High Vacuum and Ultra High Vacuum instrumentation. We can handle all instrumentation and their accessories for proper working.

We can also support/fabricate custom designed accessories for any instruments and support all special applications. We can also design and fabricate different inlets and other small instruments as per the customer’s requirement. We provide one stop solution for any typical issues by providing different hardware etc., as per the customer’s requirement.

We are into providing support services for all Analytical, Vacuum, High Vacuum and Ultra High Vacuum instrumentation. We can also supply consumables and spares for all the above supported instrumentation. We can also import and supply any special items as per the customer requirements.

We are team of experts having more than 20 years of experience in Analytical Instrumentation and also Research Instrumentation. We are also experienced working with special research instrumentation like IRMS, HR ICPMS, NGMS, MC ICPMS, CRDS etc., and are capable of maintenance.
