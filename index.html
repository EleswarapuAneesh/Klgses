KLG Scientific and Electronic Services

KLG Scientific and Electronic Services is an independent service organization with engineers who have expertise in Analytical and Mass Spectrometer Instrumentation.

We provide high-quality support services for advanced instruments such as IRMS, HR ICPMS, MC ICPMS, NGMS, TIMS, and other analytical and research tools from global manufacturers.

Based in Hyderabad, we operate across India, offering maintenance, user training, and turnkey installation support for scientific instrumentation. For any support requirements, please reach out to us via our contact section.
